# Claims

The claims of the paper that this repo tests. The first four columns were committed with the
first code, before any fitting code (commit `6a47528`), and have not changed since. The "Result" column is filled in as experiments run, including the ones that fail.

Real-data results are from two snapshots of the Deribit BTC option book taken four minutes
apart on 2026-11-14 (a Saturday evening, UTC): 374 quotes each, on 10 expiries from 2 to 313
days. They are one market state seen twice, not two independent days. Full numbers and
figures: `results/BTC_20261114T223158Z/` and `results/BTC_20261114T223559Z/`. Synthetic
results: `results/e0_synthetic/`.

| # | Claim | Paper | How we test it | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | The fit recovers a known arbitrage-free surface | implicit | Synthetic quotes from Black–Scholes and a lognormal mixture; errors against the truth for prices and density | **Holds for prices, loosely for the density.** 3 expiries × 21 strikes, half-spread 1e-4 (`tests/test_fit.py`). η = 0: quotes repriced to 1e-9. η = 0.06 and 0.25 with model strikes at most 0.02 apart: all quotes inside bid/ask; density within 1% of the true one at η = 0.25 and within 15% at η = 0.06, relative to its peak. The quotes do not pin the density down at η = 0.06: its error moved from 7% to 13% when only the LP solver's tolerance changed. With model strikes only at the quoted strikes (0.05 apart) η = 0.06 leaves bid/ask by one half-spread: the model grid has to be finer than the smoothing width |
| E1 | Linear interpolation is "too expensive" between strikes | Fig. 2 | η = 0 against η = 0.25 implied vols between quoted strikes | **Holds.** Halfway between neighbouring quoted strikes the linear fit's implied vol is above the average of its values at the two strikes at 99% of 364 midpoints (median 0.04 vol points, 90% quantile 0.26). For η = 0.25 it is above at 8–10% of them (median −0.01). Same on both snapshots |
| E2 | η trades smoothness of the density against fit | Figs. 3–5, 8 | η ∈ {0, 0.06, 0.25} on a short, a mid and a long expiry | **Holds, with no fit cost up to η = 0.25.** Local maxima of the density on the 2-, 40- and 313-day expiries: 3, 7, 3 at η = 0.06; 1, 2–3, 1 at η = 0.25. Both fit 100% of quotes inside bid/ask. The cost appears at η = 0.5 (added by us): 99.5–99.7% inside, worst error 1.2 half-spreads. The linear fit on the quoted strikes alone fits 97.6–97.9%; its misses are all at the lowest one or two quoted strikes of an expiry, and the linear model on the denser grid fits 100% |
| E3 | A full surface fits within bid/ask in under a second | §5: 91.4% inside bid/ask, median miss 21% of half-spread, under 1 s on 1000 SPX options | The same three numbers on each Deribit snapshot. The comparison is qualitative because the market differs | **Holds on a smaller problem.** η = 0.25: 100% of 374 quotes inside bid/ask, so there is no miss to measure, in 0.3 s for one LP over 10 expiries and about 1240 model strikes (desktop, including building the LP). The paper's case is 1000 quotes on 48 expiries; we did not test that size |
| E4 | Extrapolation beyond quoted strikes is sensible | Fig. 6 | Implied vols on added outer strikes | **Not reproduced.** Two at-the-money standard deviations beyond the quotes, the η = 0.25 implied vol is 6 to 16 vol points above the value at the highest quoted strike, and on the downside it is not stable: for the 2-day expiry it is 101% on one snapshot and 74% on the other, with 94% at the lowest quote on both. Outside the quotes the largest gap between η = 0, 0.06 and 0.25 is 13 to 129 vol points, depending on the expiry and the snapshot. The LP objective does not constrain the surface there |
| E5 | The surface is strictly arbitrage-free for ω = 0, and not guaranteed to be for ω = 1 | §4.2 | Independent checks on both settings; any violation found with ω = 1 is reported | **Holds.** On 20 expiries × 5000 strikes, every ω = 0 fit (η = 0, 0.06, 0.25, 0.5) violates no condition by more than 2e-11 in price. The η = 0.25 fit with ω = 1 decreases in expiry somewhere by 8e-5 and 3e-5 on the two snapshots, against a tick of 1e-4. With the LP solver at its default tolerance the ω = 0 violations were up to 2e-8, so `fit` runs it at its tightest. Synthetic ω = 0 fits pass the same checks (`tests/test_fit.py`); the synthetic ω = 1 fit happens to show no violation |
| E6 | Mid prices show arbitrage that bid/ask does not | Fig. 1, red dots | Negative mid-implied density points per expiry; feasibility of the hard bid/ask LP | **Holds.** The density implied by three neighbouring mids is negative at 43 and 46 of 354 inner strikes, on 8 of 10 expiries. The hard bid/ask LP of the linear model on the denser grid is feasible, so arbitrage-free prices exist inside bid/ask. On the quoted strikes alone it is infeasible, which is the grid and not an arbitrage. Synthetic: quotes with a calendar arbitrage make the hard LP infeasible, and the penalized fit leaves bid/ask while staying arbitrage-free |

Not tested: the generalized model (§3.4, §4.3), the discrete-local-volatility
parametrization (§3.2), smoothed linear interpolation within bid/ask (§3.3), expiries between
model expiries in the fit (§4.2 with α < 1), and extrapolation in time (eq. 15).
